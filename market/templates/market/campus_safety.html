Campus safety tips

CAMPUS-OLX is only for BBD students, but you should still stay smart while buying or selling.

When meeting for a deal

  • Always meet in a public place inside campus (canteen, library area, etc.).
  • Prefer meeting during daytime or early evening.
  • Tell a friend where you are going, or ask them to come along.
  • Check the item carefully before paying.

Money & online safety

  • Never share OTPs, passwords or full card details with anyone.
  • Be careful with UPI screenshots & payment confirmations – verify in your own app.
  • Avoid sending advance payments to unknown people.

Content & behaviour

  • Do not post illegal, stolen or restricted items.
  • Keep chats and messages respectful – remember it’s your own campus.
  • If you see something suspicious, report it to CAMPUS-OLX and college authorities.

Stay safe, be kind, and help each other. A clean marketplace depends on its users. 💚
