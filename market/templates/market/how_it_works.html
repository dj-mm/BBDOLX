How CAMPUS-OLX works

  1. Sign up with your college email.
    Only emails ending with @bbdnitm.ac.in, @bbdniit.ac.in or @bbdu.org are allowed. We verify your account using an OTP.
  2. Create an ad in a few seconds.
    Upload a clear photo, add a title, price, condition and short description. Choose the right category so buyers can find it quickly.
  3. Wait for quick approval.
    Your ad is checked for basic rules (no prohibited items, no spam). Once approved, it becomes visible to all CAMPUS-OLX users.
  4. Buyers contact you on WhatsApp.
    If you’ve added your WhatsApp number, interested buyers can ping you directly. You can also choose to hide your name & email from the ad.
  5. Meet inside campus and close the deal.
    Always meet in a safe, public place inside college and complete the deal in person. No advance payments, no sharing OTPs or bank details.
  6. Mark the item as “Sold”.
    Once your item is sold, tap Mark sold so other students don’t keep contacting you.

That’s it! Simple campus marketplace – no external users, no random calls.
