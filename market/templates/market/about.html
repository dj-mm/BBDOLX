About CAMPUS-OLX

CAMPUS-OLX is a campus-only buy & sell platform made for students of BBD group of colleges.

The goal is simple: make it super easy to buy and sell books, mobiles, laptops, hostel essentials, cycles, and more inside campus – without random outside people or sketchy deals.

  • Only verified college email IDs can create an account.
  • Every ad goes through a basic moderation step before going live.
  • Deals are done directly between students – CAMPUS-OLX just connects you.

CAMPUS-OLX is built by a student, for students – to recycle stuff, save money and make campus life a little easier. 💚
